# Customer Guide: Viewing Bills & Downloading Receipts

## Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Login
- Go to: `http://your-domain/login/`
- Enter email and password
- Complete OTP verification (if required)

### Step 2: Navigate to Bills
- Click **"My Bills"** in the sidebar menu
- Or go directly to: `/my-bills/`

### Step 3: Download Receipt
- Find your bill in the table
- Click the **[📥 Receipt]** button
- PDF downloads automatically

**Done!** You now have your receipt.

---

## What You Can See

### Your Meter Information Card
- **Meter Number** - Your unique meter ID
- **Connection Status** - Connected/Disconnected/Pending
- **Total Bills** - How many bills you have

### Bills Table
Each row shows one month's bill with:

| Column | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Month | Billing period (e.g., "February 2026") |
| Previous Reading | Last month's meter number |
| Current Reading | This month's meter number |
| Consumption | Water you used (Current - Previous) |
| Bill Amount | Cost of water (Consumption × 200 KES) |
| Penalty | Late payment charge (if any) |
| Total Due | Total amount you need to pay |
| Payment Status | Paid or Pending Payment |
| Due Date | Payment deadline |
| Action | Download receipt button |

---

## Understanding Your Bill

### Example Calculation

```
Your meter readings:
  Last month (Previous):    1,000 cubic meters
  This month (Current):     1,050 cubic meters
  
Consumption = 1,050 - 1,000 = 50 cubic meters

Bill Amount = 50 cu.m × 200 KES = 10,000 KES

If you paid late:
  Days late = 10 days
  Penalty = 10 days × 5 KES = 50 KES
  
Total you pay = 10,000 KES + 50 KES = 10,050 KES
```

### Status Badges

- **✓ Paid** (Green) - You've paid this bill
- **⏳ Pending Payment** (Yellow) - Not paid yet
- **🔴 Overdue** (Red) - Past due date

## Downloading Your Receipt

### What You Get
A PDF file with:
- Your name and meter number
- Your address
- Billing period
- Meter readings
- Water consumption
- Bill amount breakdown
- Payment status
- Due date

### How to Use Receipt
- **Keep for records** - Store for your records
- **Print** - Print to keep hard copy
- **Share** - Send to someone if needed
- **Reference** - Use when paying or contacting support

### File Name
`invoice_[BILL_ID].pdf`

Example: `invoice_1234.pdf`

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I change my bill?**
A: No, bills are set by administrators. If there's an error, contact support.

**Q: Why don't I see all my bills?**
A: Only approved bills appear here. Contact support if bills are missing.

**Q: Can I pay from here?**
A: Not directly from this page. Go to "Ongoing Bills" for payment options.

**Q: How do I dispute a bill?**
A: Contact support with your receipt and explanation.

**Q: Are receipts kept forever?**
A: Yes, all bills are stored permanently.

**Q: Can I download multiple receipts at once?**
A: Currently you download one at a time. Contact support for bulk downloads.

**Q: What if my receipt won't download?**
A: Check browser settings, try a different browser, or contact support.
